Ram’s lineup is more focused than most makes I work on — it’s a truck brand, and the catalog below reflects that, centered on the 1500 and its heavier-duty siblings across many model years.

That focus doesn’t mean the trucks themselves are simple to generalize about. Cab style, bed length, two-wheel or four-wheel drive, and engine and equipment choices all vary, and two Ram trucks from the same model year can be set up quite differently. That’s why I ask for your specific configuration before talking through a repair, rather than treating “it’s a Ram” as enough information on its own to know what’s involved.

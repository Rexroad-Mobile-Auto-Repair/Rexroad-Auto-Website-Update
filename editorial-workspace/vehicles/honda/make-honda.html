Honda is a common name on my service calls around Frisco, and the lineup covers more ground than just the Civic and Accord — SUVs like the CR-V and Pilot, the Odyssey minivan, and the Ridgeline truck are all part of the catalog below, across many model years.

Even within one model line, Honda has sold meaningfully different versions over the years — different body styles, transmissions, and in some cases hybrid powertrains alongside the standard gas engine. That’s why I ask for your specific year and model before talking through a repair, rather than assuming one Honda is like another just because the badge on the hood matches.

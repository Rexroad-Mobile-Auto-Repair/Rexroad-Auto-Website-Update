Ford is one of the most common vehicles I work on around Frisco. The lineup covers a lot of ground — compact and midsize cars, crossovers and SUVs like the Escape, Edge, Explorer, and Bronco, and full-size trucks like the F-150 and Super Duty, across many model years.

That range matters. Many of these platforms were redesigned more than once over those years, and even within the same model name, different model years can mean different engines, different electrical systems, and different parts. That’s why I ask for your specific year and model before talking through a repair, rather than treating “it’s a Ford” as enough information on its own.

Toyota covers a lot of ground on my service calls around Frisco — everything from compact and midsize cars like the Corolla and Camry, to SUVs, to trucks like the Tacoma and Tundra. The model directory below reflects that same range, spanning many model years for each.

That breadth matters. Treating “it’s a Toyota” as enough information isn’t enough for me to tell you what’s actually going on with your specific vehicle.
